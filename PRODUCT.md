## Users

Filipino travelers and families, barkadas, and group or company tour organizers, mostly in Cavite and nearby areas, who want affordable all-in trips to Asia, Europe, and the Middle East. They typically discover CheaPOX through Facebook and message before booking.

## Product Purpose

CheaPOX Travel and Tours is a travel and tour agency in Mendez, Cavite. The website's job is to get travelers to inquire about a trip. Primary action: "Inquire Now" (Messenger/Facebook). Secondary: View Packages, Call / WhatsApp +63 945 993 7084, Email Us.

## Positioning

A local, owner-run agency (Arnel V. Bael, Owner / Travel Consultant) selling all-in packages at "from" prices that are cheaper than travelers expect, summed up by the tagline "Cheap Na, Oks Na Oks Pa!" ("Cheap already, and still really good!").

- Services: domestic and international tickets, hotel reservations, tour packages, travel consultation, group and company tours, travel insurance, cruise packages, Vietnam (Da Nang) airport transfers and private tours, credit card installment payments (subject to card issuer approval).
- Packages ("from" prices exactly as on current flyers; confirm before launch): 16 packages in `src/data/packages.json`: Asia all-in in ₱ (Hong Kong, Da Nang, Shanghai, Bangkok, Indonesia, Hanoi–Sapa, Taiwan, Korea, Japan) and per-pax in $ (Dubai & Abu Dhabi, Adora Magic City Cruise, Türkiye, Amore+, Bienvenidos, Vikings, Iceland & Scandinavia).

## Product Principles

1. Every page drives to one inquiry; price clarity earns the click.
2. Show real "from" prices exactly; never invent urgency, dates, or proof.
3. Affordable must still read as trustworthy, never as cluttered flyer energy.
4. Local and reachable: the owner, the Mendez address, and direct phone lines are part of the pitch.
